Put the hero banner video here as: travel-agent-academy-hero.mp4
(or change the <source src="..."> in index.html, hero slide 1).

Tips: MP4 (H.264 video + AAC audio), 1920x1080 or smaller,
ideally under ~5-8 MB, 10-30 seconds that loop cleanly.
Until a video is added, the slide shows images/hero.jpg.

Sound: browsers only allow sound after the visitor has clicked, tapped
or pressed a key on the page. Until then the video plays muted, and
sound turns on at the first interaction (see js/hero-video.js).
